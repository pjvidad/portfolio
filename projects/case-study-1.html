The Smart Digital Notebook for Every Crocheter.

Frogless is a mobile app aimed at providing seamless project tracking and pattern management at a moment’s notice. I created the full brand identity, user interface, and icon set to turn a niche crafting need into an engaging, beautifully designed app. The result is a cohesive, modern visual ecosystem that helps crafters spend less time stressing over rows and more time bringing their creations to life.
