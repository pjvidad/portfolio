Hey! I’m Princess—glad you made it here.

Consider this our official introduction! I’m a designer and illustrator who turns big concepts into bold, functional visual pieces, ranging from digital graphics and interactive interfaces to fun character art. Equipped with a computer science foundation, I treat design as a blend of logic and pure creative freedom.

That dual focus has allowed me to present international research, craft startup media, and explore interactive design in game engines. At the end of the day, I just love making things that look great and feel meaningful.

Beyond the screen, I’m usually sketching, making music on the piano, crocheting, or trying out a new art medium. Thanks for visiting my corner of the internet. Take a look around and enjoy!
